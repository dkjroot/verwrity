Verwrity reads this replaceable local file with its own small MyThes-format reader.
It does not bundle or depend on LibreOffice or the MyThes software library.
Sense groups carry part-of-speech labels and alternatives, not definitions.
Antonyms, broader (generic) terms, related terms and similar terms retain their
source relation labels. Lookup does not infer context or inflect alternatives.
No runtime downloads, external requests, telemetry or AI calls are needed.
